# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Windows users who want screenshots, game clips, phone exports, scans, or other personal media folders backed up to Google Photos without operating a command line. The primary workflow is checking that protection is active, adding or maintaining source folders, and occasionally starting a manual run.

## Product Purpose

Provide a small native Windows application that makes Google Photos folder backup understandable and dependable. Success means users can configure their own folders inside the app, see exactly what is protected, and trust that unchanged or already-known media is not uploaded again.

## Positioning

Content-aware Google Photos backup for Windows in a native, low-overhead tray application.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and quietly capable. Copy is direct and reassuring without exposing internal implementation details unless a diagnostic action needs them.

## Anti-references

No Electron-style oversized shell, generic SaaS dashboard, decorative gradients, fake glass, excessive cards, unexplained technical jargon, or fixed personal paths disguised as product defaults.

## Design Principles

- Make protection status and the next useful action obvious.
- Treat folder management as a first-class workflow, not a configuration-file escape hatch.
- Preserve native Windows behavior for moving, focusing, opening, and closing the app.
- Expose powerful maintenance actions progressively while keeping the primary view calm.
- Keep every background action transparent about what changed and what was skipped.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong monochrome contrast, keyboard-operable actions, visible focus, 44-pixel minimum primary targets, clear German copy, and layouts that leave room for longer translated labels.
